Timing

Lower thirds animate in over about 1 to 1.5 seconds, hold for 4 to 5 seconds, then animate out in 0.5 seconds. Logo intros run about 3 to 4 seconds before the cut.

Vertical safe zones

The default 9:16 position sits above the bottom 470 px and clear of the right 150 px, where Instagram and TikTok draw captions and buttons. Tick Safe zones to check after moving a lower third.

Brand

Every style uses the chasingthesash.com palette and type: black, cream, pink #FF1F6E and gold #C6993A, with Cormorant Garamond and Montserrat.
