Fashions on the Field

Where fashion
is a competitive
sport

Fashions on the Field is a passionate subculture held at Australian racetracks, where contestants showcase style, creativity, and individuality. Judged on originality, elegance, and attention to detail — the coveted winner's sash is everything.

More than a style contest, it's a world where women who feel unseen in everyday life become superstars on the stage. Bold characters, fierce rivalries, and moments of deep vulnerability and unexpected connection.

Series One

From red dust
to Royal Randwick

Australia is home to over 400 racetracks — many set in remote regional communities, each with its own distinct look, feel, and flair. In country towns, Cup Day is the highlight of the social calendar.

Series one journeys from tropical Cairns to the outback dust of Birdsville, through thoroughbred country at Scone, across the wild west of Dubbo — culminating in a glittering finale at Royal Randwick on Melbourne Cup Day.
