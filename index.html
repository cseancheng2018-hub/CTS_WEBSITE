About Us

Where fashion
is a competitive
sport

Chasing the Sash follows Fashions on the Field — a passionate subculture held at Australian racetracks, where contestants showcase style, creativity, and individuality. Judged on originality, elegance, and attention to detail — the coveted winner's sash is everything.

More than a style contest, it's a world where women who feel unseen in everyday life become superstars on the stage. Bold characters, fierce rivalries, and moments of deep vulnerability and unexpected connection.
